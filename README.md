# REIMEI Civilization OS / Next Civilization OS

## A Conceptual Architecture for a Civilization Compatible with Natural Law, Planetary Circulation, and the Age of AI

---

## Abstract

**REIMEI Civilization OS**, also described as the **Next Civilization OS**, is a conceptual architecture for redesigning civilization so that it becomes compatible with natural law, planetary circulation, the biosphere, urban infrastructure, technology, and artificial intelligence.

The term **REIMEI** means “dawn” in Japanese.
It represents the beginning of a new civilization model after the limits of the current civilization operating system have become visible.

Modern civilization faces interconnected crises: climate change, water scarcity, food insecurity, soil degradation, forest loss, ocean ecosystem decline, energy instability, urban overheating, social fragmentation, and the rapid expansion of AI.

These are often treated as separate problems.
However, this document interprets them as symptoms of a deeper structural failure: the current Civilization OS is not compatible with the circular structure of the Earth system.

The current Civilization OS has been shaped by:

* Linear thinking
* Consumption-based development
* Dualism between humanity and nature
* Anthropocentrism
* Growth-centered economics
* Externalization of nature
* Short-term optimization
* AI optimization without sufficient civilizational purpose

The **Next Civilization OS** proposes a shift from growth-centered civilization to circulation-centered civilization, from domination of nature to natural complementarity, and from AI as a tool of acceleration to AI as a support intelligence for civilization tuning.

The foundation of REIMEI Civilization OS is the **Six Principles of Natural Law**:

* Natural Law / Providence
* Harmony
* Circulation
* Structure
* Order
* Wa

These principles provide a compatibility framework for evaluating whether policies, technologies, cities, infrastructures, and AI systems can support a sustainable and regenerative civilization.

## 4. Core Problems of the Current Civilization OS

The current Civilization OS contains several structural problems.

---

### 4.1 Linear Thinking

Modern civilization often operates through a linear model:

* Extract
* Produce
* Distribute
* Consume
* Discard

This model was useful for industrial production.

However, the Earth is not a linear system.

The Earth is a circular system.

Water circulates.
Carbon circulates.
Nitrogen circulates.
Organic matter decomposes and returns to soil.
Oceans transport heat, oxygen, and nutrients.
Forests support rainfall, groundwater, and climate stability.
Microorganisms regenerate soil.

Nature is not a one-way machine.

It is a multilayered feedback system.

If civilization is designed through linear thinking, waste, pollution, resource depletion, and ecological destruction become structural outcomes.

---

### 4.2 Consumption-Based Civilization

The current Civilization OS often treats consumption growth as development.

More production.
More purchasing.
More usage.
More disposal.
Faster replacement.

This model can produce short-term economic growth.

However, the Earth has limits.

Water, soil, forests, oceans, biodiversity, minerals, and atmospheric stability are not infinite.

The problem is not consumption itself.

The problem is that materials and energy after consumption are not returned to regenerative cycles.

Instead, they accumulate as waste, pollution, heat, degradation, and ecological burden.

A civilization that consumes without regeneration cannot remain stable in the long term.

---

### 4.3 Dualism

The current Civilization OS often separates humanity from nature.

This appears in many forms:

* Humanity vs. nature
* Cities vs. forests
* Economy vs. environment
* Technology vs. life
* Artificial systems vs. ecosystems

However, human civilization does not exist outside nature.

Human breathing depends on plants and oceans.
Food depends on soil, water, microorganisms, insects, and ecosystems.
Cities depend on water sources, farmland, energy, and climate stability.
Economic activity ultimately depends on the productive capacity of the natural world.

Human civilization is not above nature.

It exists inside natural circulation.

A dualistic Civilization OS causes civilization to forget its own foundations.

---

### 4.4 Anthropocentrism

The current Civilization OS has placed human benefit, convenience, comfort, and economic growth at the center.

This produced remarkable short-term development.

However, it contains a serious long-term contradiction.

There is no Earth where only humans can prosper.

If forests weaken, water circulation weakens.
If soil degrades, food production becomes unstable.
If oceans weaken, climate regulation and marine ecosystems weaken.
If biodiversity declines, ecosystem resilience declines.

Anthropocentrism ultimately destabilizes the survival conditions of humanity itself.

The Next Civilization OS does not deny humanity.

It repositions humanity correctly within the biosphere and natural law.

---

### 4.5 The AI Objective-Setting Problem

AI can optimize given objectives at high speed and large scale.

However, if the objective is wrong, AI will reinforce the wrong Civilization OS.

If the objective is consumption expansion, AI will optimize consumption.
If the objective is short-term profit, AI will optimize short-term profit.
If the objective is surveillance and control, AI will strengthen surveillance and control.

The problem of AI is not only inside AI itself.

The deeper question is:

**What Civilization OS is AI connected to?**

What does AI learn as success?
What is AI asked to optimize?
What values define its objective function?

This is one of the central questions of civilization design in the age of AI.

## 7. The Six Principles of Natural Law

REIMEI Civilization OS is founded on the **Six Principles of Natural Law**.

These principles are the basic criteria by which civilization can be evaluated for long-term compatibility with natural law.

The Six Principles are:

* Natural Law / Providence
* Harmony
* Circulation
* Structure
* Order
* Wa

Regeneration and coexistence are not separate principles in this framework.

They are outcomes that emerge when civilization is designed in accordance with the Six Principles.

---

## 8. First Principle: Natural Law / Providence

The first principle is **Natural Law**, also described as **Providence**.

Civilization cannot exist outside natural law.

Water flows from high places to low places.
Heat moves from higher temperature to lower temperature.
Life exists through cycles of energy and matter.
Soil is formed through microorganisms, organic matter, water, plants, and time.
Oceans support the planet through heat, oxygen, nutrients, and life cycles.

Human technology can assist nature.

However, civilization cannot be built by ignoring natural law.

In REIMEI Civilization OS, natural law is placed at the highest level of civilization design.

Technology should not be used to dominate nature.

It should be used to understand natural law, complement natural processes, and align civilization within the conditions that make life possible.

---

## 9. Second Principle: Harmony

The second principle is **Harmony**.

Nature is not sustained by a single element.

Forests, soil, microorganisms, insects, rivers, oceans, wind, clouds, sunlight, and biodiversity support one another.

Civilization is the same.

Water alone is not enough.
Food alone is not enough.
Energy alone is not enough.
Cities alone are not enough.
AI alone is not enough.

If AI becomes advanced but is not harmonized with natural circulation and human society, it can become dangerous.

Harmony does not mean making everything identical.

Harmony means creating a relationship in which different elements maintain their roles while supporting the stability of the whole.

Humanity and nature.
Cities and forests.
Technology and life.
AI and humans.
Economy and ecosystems.
Present generations and future generations.

REIMEI Civilization OS treats these not as opposing forces, but as complementary relationships.

---

## 10. Third Principle: Circulation

The third principle is **Circulation**.

In nature, there is no absolute waste.

Fallen leaves return to soil.
Microorganisms decompose organic matter.
Plants absorb nutrients.
Life returns to the cycle.
Water evaporates, becomes clouds, falls as rain, flows through rivers, and returns to the ocean.

Nature is sustained by circulation.

Modern civilization has disconnected many materials from circulation.

Extract.
Produce.
Consume.
Discard.

This linear structure behaves as if Earth were infinite.

But Earth is a closed circular system.

REIMEI Civilization OS requires water, organic matter, heat, resources, food, and energy to be returned to circulation as much as possible.

Examples include:

* Returning food waste and fallen leaves to soil
* Treating and reusing wastewater
* Using waste heat
* Transforming cities into circulation devices
* Connecting agriculture with soil regeneration
* Integrating industrial waste into resource circulation

The principle of Circulation reconnects civilization to the circular structure of nature.

---

## 11. Fourth Principle: Structure

The fourth principle is **Structure**.

Problems are not caused only by isolated events.

They often arise from structure.

Food loss is not only a matter of individual awareness.
It is also a structural problem created by mass production, mass distribution, mass sales, and mass disposal.

Water scarcity is not only a lack of rain.
It is connected to forests, groundwater, urban pavement, agriculture, river management, and climate change.

Climate change is not only an energy problem.
It is connected to forests, soil, oceans, cities, agriculture, consumption structures, and economic ideology.

The principle of Structure means looking not only at symptoms but also at the systems behind them.

Modern civilization tends to divide problems into sectors:

* Environmental problems
* Agricultural problems
* Water problems
* Energy problems
* Urban problems
* AI problems

But in reality, these are interconnected.

REIMEI Civilization OS designs civilization structure itself.

Water, food, energy, cities, AI, and natural circulation should not be treated as separate fields.

They should be integrated as one civilization infrastructure.

---

## 12. Fifth Principle: Order

The fifth principle is **Order**.

Order does not mean domination or authoritarian control.

In this framework, Order means the arrangement and relationship through which natural law, the biosphere, society, technology, and AI can function without collapse.

Nature has order.

Day and night.
Seasonal cycles.
Water flow.
Food webs.
Soil formation.
Ocean currents.
Ecological balance.

This order is not static.

It is dynamic order maintained through change.

Civilization also needs order.

Where does water come from, and where does it return?
How is food produced, and how is it circulated?
Where is energy generated, and how is it used?
How far should cities expand, and how should they connect with nature?
What should AI be used for, and what values should guide its decisions?

Without order, civilization moves toward uncontrolled consumption and expansion.

Order in REIMEI Civilization OS is not a system for restricting human life.

It is the structural arrangement required for civilization to remain compatible with natural law over the long term.

---

## 13. Sixth Principle: Wa

The sixth principle is **Wa**.

Wa is a Japanese concept often translated as harmony, but in this framework it has a deeper meaning.

Wa does not mean simple peace or compromise.

Wa means a state in which different elements do not erase one another, but maintain their differences while forming a coherent whole.

The Wa of nature is supported by diversity.

Forests contain many kinds of plants.
Soil contains countless microorganisms.
Rivers contain fish, insects, plants, and sediments.
Oceans contain life from plankton to large marine animals.

A stable ecosystem cannot be built from only one type of existence.

Civilization is the same.

Humanity alone is not enough.
AI alone is not enough.
Cities alone are not enough.
Economy alone is not enough.
Nature isolated from human society is also not enough.

The principle of Wa guides humanity, nature, AI, technology, cities, and the biosphere toward resonance rather than opposition.

In REIMEI Civilization OS, Wa is the final integrative principle.

By understanding Natural Law, maintaining Harmony, restoring Circulation, designing Structure, and establishing Order, civilization can move toward Wa between humanity and the biosphere.
