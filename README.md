## Abstract

**REIMEI Civilization OS**, also described as the **Next Civilization OS**, is a conceptual architecture for redesigning civilization so that it becomes compatible with natural law, planetary circulation, the biosphere, urban infrastructure, technology, and artificial intelligence.

The term **REIMEI** means “dawn” in Japanese.  
It represents the beginning of a new civilization model after the limits of the current civilization operating system have become visible.

Modern civilization faces interconnected crises: climate change, water scarcity, food insecurity, soil degradation, forest loss, ocean ecosystem decline, energy instability, urban overheating, social fragmentation, and the rapid expansion of AI.

These are often treated as separate problems.  
However, this document interprets them as symptoms of a deeper structural failure: the current Civilization OS is not compatible with the circular structure of the Earth system.

The current Civilization OS has been shaped by:

* Linear thinking
* Consumption-based development
* Dualism between humanity and nature
* Anthropocentrism
* Growth-centered economics
* Externalization of nature
* Short-term optimization
* AI optimization without sufficient civilizational purpose

The **Next Civilization OS** proposes a shift from growth-centered civilization to circulation-centered civilization, from domination of nature to natural complementarity, and from AI as a tool of acceleration to AI as a support intelligence for civilization tuning.

The foundation of REIMEI Civilization OS is the **Six Principles of Natural Law**:

* Natural Law / Providence
* Harmony
* Circulation
* Structure
* Order
* Wa

These principles provide a compatibility framework for evaluating whether policies, technologies, cities, infrastructures, and AI systems can support a sustainable and regenerative civilization.

## 1. What Is the Next Civilization OS?

The **Next Civilization OS** is a foundational framework for redesigning human civilization so that it no longer contradicts natural law, planetary circulation, the biosphere, cities, technology, and artificial intelligence.

Here, “OS” does not mean software in the narrow technical sense.

It refers to the invisible operating principles that determine how civilization functions.

A computer operating system coordinates applications, hardware, memory, data, input, output, and user interaction.

In a similar way, a Civilization OS coordinates:

* Worldview
* Value systems
* Economic structures
* Social institutions
* Science and technology
* Urban infrastructure
* Water systems
* Food systems
* Energy systems
* Waste systems
* Information systems
* AI decision systems
* The relationship between humanity and nature
* Responsibility to future generations
